Chef Br1ght's Golden Brown Perfection

⏱️ Cook Time: 45 Mins 🔥 Difficulty: Medium

Secret Spice Blend | 350°F Mastery | Maximum Crunch

The Ingredients (Tap to Check)

  • 🧄 Garlic & Onion Powder
  • 💨 Smoked Paprika
  • 🔥 Classic Paprika
  • 🧂 Salt & Black Pepper
  • 🌿 Dried Thyme
  • 🥚 Fresh Egg Wash
  • 🌾 All-Purpose Flour
  • 🔥 High-Heat Cooking Oil

The Chef's Method

1. Spice the Flour: Mix your flour with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, thyme, and both types of paprika. This is where the color comes from!

2. The Double Coat: Dip your chicken into the Egg Wash first, let the excess drip, and then roll it heavily in your seasoned flour mix.

3. Precision Heat: Heat your cooking oil to exactly 350°F. If the oil is too cold, it gets greasy; too hot, and it burns!

4. The Gold-Brown Fry: Fry the chicken until the crust is a deep golden brown. The smoked paprika will give it a beautiful, rich "pro" look.

5. Final Rest: Let the chicken sit for 3-5 minutes on a wire rack. This keeps the skin "yuyyyy" crispy and helps the juices stay inside!

Best Glazed Chicken Tenders

⏱️ Cook Time: 25 Mins 🔥 Difficulty: Easy

Homemade Crispy Tenders | Sweet & Savory Sticky Glaze | Perfectly Calm & Simple

The Ingredients (Tap to Check)

  • 🐔 Chicken Breast Slices (Strips)
  • 🧄 Minced Garlic
  • 🧂 Salt & Black Pepper
  • 🍋 Fresh Lime Juice
  • 🌾 All-Purpose Flour
  • 🧈 Knorr Chicken Seasoning Cube
  • 🥚 Beaten Eggs
  • 🍅 Tomato Paste / Chili Sauce
  • 🪵 Dark Soy Sauce
  • 🌻 Cooking Oil (for frying)

The Chef's Method

1. Marinate the Tenders: Place your fresh chicken breast strips into a bowl. Add a scoop of fresh minced garlic, a shake of salt, plenty of ground black pepper, and squeeze the juice of a fresh lime straight over them. Hand-mix it thoroughly until fully absorbed.

2. Prepare the Flour Coating: In a separate bowl, add your all-purpose flour. Crumble a Knorr Chicken Seasoning Cube over the flour and add an extra pinch of black pepper, then whisk it all completely together.

3. Coat the Chicken: Whisk up your eggs in a shallow dish. Dip each seasoned chicken tender strip straight into the egg wash, then roll it into the chicken-seasoned flour until fully coated.

4. Fry to Golden Perfection: Pour your cooking oil into a pan and heat it. Carefully lower your tenders into the hot oil and deep-fry them smoothly until they develop a crisp, gorgeous golden brown crust. Once cooked, set aside.

5. Toss in the Homemade Glaze: Heat a separate frying pan over your burner. Pour in tomato sauce/paste, dark soy sauce, and a touch of extra seasoning to build a thick bubbling glaze. Drop your crispy cooked tenders directly into the glaze, toss it with a wooden spoon until sticky and coated, and top with fresh herbs to finish!
